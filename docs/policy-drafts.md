## Current product boundary to preserve

Public browsing and the fixed two-card Events sample require no sign-in. The sample has a five-second timer and repeats the same pair; it does not run a paid event. Full host controls are development-only at this audit baseline. Sign-in, purchasing and verified provider fulfillment remain unavailable until secure integration acceptance.

The intended host service is Discord-authenticated at Buy, product play-count Slots rather than stored money, manual host judgment and manual winner display names. No participant accounts, attendance tracking or bot. Names are not verified Discord identities. Connected session maximum four hours and same-session resume without another Slot debit are owner decisions; unresolved time/expiry/refund semantics must not be filled with old assumptions.

Persistent Game History is approved product direction, not a claim that storage is currently delivered or immune to lawful deletion. Describe only the deployed data behavior in the eventual public policy.

## Draft C — Purchase, cancellation and refund policy

**INTERNAL OUTLINE — NO COMMERCIAL PROMISE APPROVED**

Prepare bilingual final wording only after the owner supplies:

- Seller identity, approved products, currency, actual price mapping, taxes and invoice responsibility.
- Exactly when an entitlement is fulfilled and when it counts as used.
- Slot validity/expiry and treatment of unused, started, interrupted, failed, canceled or duplicate purchases.
- Eligibility and procedure for cancellation/refund and dispute escalation, reconciled with applicable rights.
- Provider responsibilities, review authority and actual processing expectations. No invented processing-day promise.
- Fraud/chargeback/reversal behavior and effect on unused access, active sessions and immutable ledger history.
- Treatment of platform outages and cases where payment is confirmed but fulfillment fails.
- Support/contact route and evidence a customer should provide without sending sensitive payment data.

Until implemented and approved, public copy must state purchasing is unavailable. Do not advertise “all sales final”, guaranteed refunds, automatic credit compensation, a tax percentage or a price without approval.
